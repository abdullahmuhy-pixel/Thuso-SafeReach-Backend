SafeReach lets you start a check-in timer before a trip. If you do not confirm you are safe in time, our coordinators are alerted automatically, even if your phone is off.

What SafeReach does

Everything below works from the app on your phone or from your account on this website.

⏱️

Check-in timer

Choose a duration and destination. Tap I'm safe when you arrive, or extend the time.

🔔

Automatic escalation

If time runs out, the server raises an SOS alert for you. It does not need your phone to be on.

🆘

One-tap SOS

Send an alert with your location. In the app you can also shake your phone, with a 5 second cancel.

📝

Incident reports

Report theft, assault, accidents, fires and medical events so coordinators can respond and spot patterns.

📶

Works offline

In the app, alerts and reports made without signal are saved and sent when you are back online.

🧭

Trip tools

The app also has a trip planner, medical ID and a phrasebook that work on your phone.

How it works

Three steps. No special equipment.

1

Create an account

Use your phone number and a password. Do it once on this site or in the app.

2

Start a check-in before you travel

Pick 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes and say where you are going.

3

Confirm you are safe

Tap I'm safe. If you do not, a coordinator from the Thuso branch sees the alert on their dashboard.

NGO staff: sign in to the coordinator and admin dashboard.
